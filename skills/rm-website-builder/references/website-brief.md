# RM Group Strategies LLC Website Brief

Use this reference when implementing content, pages, navigation, lead forms, or brand details for the RM Group Strategies LLC website.

## Company positioning

RM Group Strategies LLC is a Nevada-based parent company specializing in government contracting, transportation, fleet services, construction, remodeling, logistics, equipment rental, procurement solutions, and business consulting.

The website should resemble a Fortune 500 government contractor and be optimized for lead generation and procurement opportunities.
